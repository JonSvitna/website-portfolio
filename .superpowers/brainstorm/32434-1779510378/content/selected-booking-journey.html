Selected Direction: Cinematic Booking Journey

This direction uses motion to guide visitors from first impression to booking while rebuilding the hero as live, clickable layers.

Ryan Motivates Book Now
From request to arrival.

Design Intent

  • Rebuild the hero so nav, headline, service cards, and booking actions are real clickable UI.
  • Add bold but polished motion: staged hero entrance, route-like transitions, card depth, and scroll-driven reveals.
  • Make the page feel like a guided booking experience, not only a brochure.
  • Keep the luxury champagne/parchment brand, BMW emphasis, and realistic people imagery.
